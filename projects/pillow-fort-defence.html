Pillow Fort Defence

Six rooms, six nights. Keep the bedtime monsters out of the pillow fort until morning.

Each room is harder than the one before. Stars are saved in this browser. Turn your phone sideways for a bigger board.

How to play

  • Teddy Tosser throws cotton balls at one monster at a time.
  • Bubble Wand slows monsters on the ground. It can’t reach flying bats.
  • Night-Light pulses at everything nearby, and its glow reveals hidden monsters.
  • Monsters that reach the fort cost hearts. Lose all 20 and the night is over.
  • Survive every wave. Grumble the Laundry Mountain arrives last.

Tip: toys placed in the gap between two stretches of rug reach monsters twice as they wind past.
